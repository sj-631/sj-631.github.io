I am Shen Jia (Jason), a final-year PhD student in Statistics at Trinity College Dublin, where I was awarded the Trinity Doctorate Research Scholarship in 2023. I work under the supervision of [Dr James Ng](https://www.tcd.ie/scss/people/academic-staff/ngja/) and [Dr David Selby](https://mlgh.net/author/david-selby/), with coordination from [Professor Mark Little](https://www.tcd.ie/research/profiles/?profile=MLITTLE).

My research focuses mainly on clustering and its potential applications in the study of ANCA-associated vasculitis.

Before my PhD, I completed an MPhil in Population Health Sciences (Health Data Science track) at the University of Cambridge, supported by a £14,000 Health Data Research UK scholarship. I hold a BSc in Applied Mathematics from Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University and the University of Liverpool, and was a 2021 School of Science graduate representative at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. You can read my [student profile on the university's website](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/zh/news/2021/06/xuezigushijiashen) (in Chinese).
