I am Shen Jia (Jason), a final-year PhD student in Statistics at Trinity College Dublin, where I was awarded the Trinity Doctorate Research Scholarship in 2023. I work under the supervision of James Ng, with coordination from Mark Little. Our research focuses on clustering, mixture models, and their applications to ANCA-associated vasculitis.

Before my PhD, I completed an MPhil at the University of Cambridge, supported by a Health Data Research UK scholarship (14k). I studied Applied Mathematics at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University and the University of Liverpool for my undergraduate education, and was a 2021 School of Science graduate representative at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. You can read my [student profile on the university's website](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/zh/news/2021/06/xuezigushijiashen) (in Chinese).
